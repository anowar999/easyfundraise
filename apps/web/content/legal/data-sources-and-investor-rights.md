# Data Sources and Investor Rights

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

**Where our investor information comes from.** EasyFundraise's investor directory is compiled from publicly available professional sources: venture firm and fund websites and team pages, press releases and news coverage of investments, public conference and event listings, public professional profiles and bios, podcasts and published interviews, and public regulatory or company filings. We also use web research to summarise an investor's publicly stated investment focus. We do not access information behind logins or paywalls, and we do not collect personal-life information.

**What we store.** Professional fields only: name, role, firm, professional contact details and public profile links, location (city/country), investment focus, stages and typical check size where publicly stated, public portfolio and public statements of thesis, and a professional photo where the firm publishes one. Each record notes its sources and when it was last reviewed.

**How it is used.** Founders use the directory to identify relevant investors and to send a limited number of individually written outreach emails from their own email account. We enforce daily limits, require an opt-out line in every message, and remove investors who opt out.

**Your rights as an investor.** You can at any time:
- **View** the information we hold about you.
- **Correct** it (e.g. new firm, new focus).
- **Opt out** of outreach from EasyFundraise users.
- **Remove** your profile entirely.
Use [the investor request form](/investor-request) or email privacy@easyfundraise.com. We action requests within 72 hours and keep a suppression record so your details are not re-added. You may also contact your data-protection authority.

**Aggregated engagement signals.** To help founders approach investors respectfully, we compute aggregated, anonymised indicators (for example, how responsive investors on the platform tend to be). These never reveal which founders contacted you and are only shown when based on a minimum number of independent workspaces.
