# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Effective: 31 October 2026_

This Policy explains how EasyFundraise uses cookies and similar technologies on easyfundraise.com and in the EasyFundraise application.

## Cookies we use

We use only **strictly necessary** cookies. They make the Service work and do not need your consent under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

- **ef_session**: keeps you signed in. Secure, http-only, expires after 30 days of inactivity.
- **Sign-in state**: a short-lived cookie that protects the Google sign-in and Gmail connection from forgery. Deleted after sign-in.
- **ef_room_…**: remembers that you unlocked a founder's data room, so you are not asked again. Expires after 30 days.

We also store your selected workspace in your browser's local storage so the app opens where you left it.

## What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or third-party analytics cookies. We do not sell data collected through cookies.

## Emails sent through EasyFundraise

Emails founders send through EasyFundraise may contain a small image that tells the sender when the email was opened. Founders can turn this off. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for details.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies you will not be able to sign in.
