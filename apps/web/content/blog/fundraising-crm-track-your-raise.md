A fundraise is a sales process with a deadline. You will talk to dozens of investors in parallel, each at a different point, and the round closes faster when nobody goes cold.

## The nine stages that work

1. Not started
2. Intro requested
3. In contact
4. First call arranged
5. Deck requested
6. Deep dive or due diligence
7. Term sheet or offer
8. Signed
9. Payment received

Every investor sits in exactly one stage. Move them forward the moment something happens.

## The fields worth tracking

- Priority (P1 to P4)
- Can they lead?
- Ticket size and agreed amount
- Who introduced you
- Next contact date
- Notes from every call

## Habits that keep the round moving

- **Review your funnel every morning.** Who is due a follow-up today?
- **Write notes straight after each call,** with the next step and a date.
- **Send round updates** when you secure a lead or pass 50% committed.

## Why not a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets do not remind you, do not know when an investor replied, and are painful on a phone. A fundraising CRM like EasyFundraise turns notes into reminders, moves a card when an investor replies, and works on your phone between meetings.
