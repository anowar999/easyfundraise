Investors read cold emails. Many of the best seed deals start with one. What they do not read is a 400-word wall of text with a deck attached and no reason why it landed in their inbox.

## The rules that matter

- **Keep the first email under 120 words.** Follow-ups under 80.
- **One specific reason you chose them.** A portfolio company, a thesis, something they wrote.
- **One line on what you do, one line of proof.** Numbers beat adjectives.
- **One small ask.** Twenty minutes, or "can I send the deck?"
- **No attachments in the first email.** Offer the deck instead.
- **Send from your own email address,** on Tuesday to Thursday morning in their time zone.

## Template 1: the thesis echo (pre-seed and seed)

**Subject:** Clinicly x Northwind

Hi Grace,

Your post on clinician burnout is why I'm writing.

Clinicly is AI triage that gives GPs back two hours a day. We're live in 48 clinics, at £38K MRR and growing 14% a month.

We're raising a $2M seed round. Open to a 20-minute call next week?

If this isn't relevant, reply "no" and I won't follow up.

Maya

## Template 2: the portfolio bridge (angels)

**Subject:** From one Medibright fan to another

Hi Sam,

You backed Medibright early. We're solving the step right after theirs: what happens once a patient is triaged.

Three months in, 12 clinics are paying us and two NHS trusts are piloting.

Would you be open to a quick look at our deck?

## Template 3: the momentum update (follow-up)

**Subject:** Re: Clinicly x Northwind

Hi Grace,

Quick update: we closed our lead this week and the round is 60% committed.

Happy to share the deck and data room if helpful before we close.

## How many follow-ups?

Two, spaced four to seven days apart, each adding something new: a metric, a customer, a milestone. Stop the moment they reply, even if the reply is "not now". EasyFundraise does this automatically: follow-ups thread under your original email and stop on any reply.

## Deliverability: stay out of spam

- Warm up a new mailbox slowly: start at five emails a day and grow over two weeks.
- Never send more than a few emails a day to the same firm.
- Avoid link shorteners, images and spammy words like "guaranteed".
- Always include a simple way to opt out.
